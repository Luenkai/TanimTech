A BETTER FIELD PHOTO

Keep the soil in focus

01

Use natural daylightAvoid harsh shadows, flash and color filters.
02

Fill the frame with soilUse a small representative sample on a plain surface; remove large leaves and debris.
03

Hold steady, shoot closeKeep the camera parallel to the sample and tap to focus. Avoid blurry detail.

A clearer image improves visibility, not the reliability of a soil test.

⚠  A photo has limits

Model review can describe visible traits, but cannot reliably determine soil texture, pH, nutrients, drainage, organic matter or chemistry. Keep measured details as separate inputs.
